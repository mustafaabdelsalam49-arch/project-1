About Us

About the Corporation

Al-Majal Al-Amin Safety Systems Corporation was established to become a leading company in this field, offering the latest solutions, products, and services. We provide a wide and diverse range of safety, early warning, and firefighting systems.

Al-Majal Al-Amin develops, markets, and provides technical support for multiple levels of integrated solution technologies in a manner that suits and aligns with the needs of organizations, companies, and large, medium, and small enterprises.

Vision

Serving our nation to be a safe place to live and work in accordance with the Kingdom's directions and vision.

Mission

Protecting lives and properties from fires. Providing advice and instructions regarding fire protection measures and fire hazards. We also work on educating the community and enhancing public awareness about fire safety.

Our Values & Principles

Development & Innovation

Al-Majal Al-Amin Safety Systems Corporation keeps pace with the latest developments in firefighting to suit the diverse needs of companies, organizations, and authorities, along with the necessary services, maintenance, and technical support.

Clients & Information

Al-Majal Al-Amin's staff has been trained to maintain the confidentiality of our clients' information. Making our clients happy and satisfied by achieving their goals is a genuine value shared by all members of the company.

Reliability & Services

We understand the importance of product quality, so we are committed to excellence. Our products are easy to install and scalable. Al-Majal Al-Amin offers maintenance and technical support contracts at the company or the client's site.

Project Management Office

The Project Management Office organizes and monitors the company's projects through global methodologies and accumulated experience among a group of experienced and competent project experts. The office provides comprehensive reports to the management for development purposes and to the client for reassurance on the project's progress.

Training

Al-Majal Al-Amin develops the administrative and technical staff, which positively reflects on the actual performance with our clients. We prepare an annual package of courses in coordination with major specialized international institutions.
